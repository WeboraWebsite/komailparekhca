Frequently Asked Questions

At what point should I consider debt collection services?

+

While there's no one-size-fits-all answer, it's generally advisable to consider professional debt collection services when invoices remain unpaid 60-90 days beyond their due date, especially after your internal collection efforts have been exhausted. Early intervention often yields better results, so don't wait too long to address significant outstanding receivables. We can help assess your specific situation and recommend the best timing for intervention.

How do you maintain client relationships during the collection process?

+

We employ a professional, respectful approach that emphasizes problem-solving rather than confrontation. Our communications are always professional, and we aim to understand the debtor's circumstances while still advocating for our client's interests. We focus on finding solutions that allow debtors to meet their obligations without burning bridges. This balanced approach often preserves business relationships while still achieving successful recovery outcomes.

What information do you need to begin the collection process?

+

To initiate the collection process effectively, we typically require copies of invoices, contracts or agreements, proof of delivery or service completion, records of any payments made, documentation of previous collection attempts, contact information for the debtor, and any relevant correspondence history. The more complete the documentation you can provide, the more effective our collection efforts will be. We'll guide you through the specific requirements for your situation.

What are your fees for debt collection services?

+

Our fee structure typically operates on a contingency basis, meaning we receive a percentage of the amount successfully collected. This percentage varies based on factors such as the age of the debt, amount outstanding, and complexity of the case. We may also offer fixed fee arrangements for certain situations. We're transparent about our pricing structure and will clearly explain all fees before beginning any collection activities. This alignment of interests ensures we're motivated to maximize your recovery.
