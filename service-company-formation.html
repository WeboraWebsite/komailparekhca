Frequently Asked Questions

What are the main differences between mainland, free zone, and offshore companies?

+

Mainland companies can conduct business throughout the UAE without restrictions but typically require a local sponsor. Free zone companies offer 100% foreign ownership, tax benefits, and simplified procedures but have some restrictions on mainland business activities. Offshore companies are ideal for international operations, asset holding, and tax planning but cannot conduct business within the UAE.

How long does it typically take to set up a company in the UAE?

+

The timeline varies depending on the type of company and jurisdiction. Generally, free zone companies can be established within 1-2 weeks, mainland companies within 2-4 weeks, and offshore companies within 1-3 weeks. However, these timelines can vary based on the complexity of your business and the specific requirements of the jurisdiction.

What are the minimum capital requirements for company formation in the UAE?

+

Capital requirements vary based on the jurisdiction and type of company. Many free zones have minimal or no capital requirements, while mainland companies may require a minimum capital based on the type of license. Offshore companies typically have nominal capital requirements. We can provide specific information based on your chosen business structure and location.

Can I establish a UAE company without being physically present?

+

Yes, in many cases we can handle the entire company formation process without requiring your physical presence in the UAE. Through power of attorney arrangements and digital documentation, we can manage the registration process on your behalf, though some jurisdictions may require signatures on certain documents.
