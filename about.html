About the Founder

Meet the visionary behind Komail Parekh Chartered Accountants LLC

Komail H. Parekh

Founder and Chairman

Chartered Accountant (ICAI) Bachelor of General Laws (BGL) MOE Registered Auditor

Komail H. Parekh is a seasoned finance professional with a career spanning over three decades. As a qualified Chartered Accountant from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), he also holds a Bachelor of General Laws (BGL) degree from the prestigious Government Law College, Mumbai.

Mr. Parekh brings a wealth of experience from both industry and public practice in the UAE. He served as the Finance Manager for 17 years at a leading electromechanical company, part of a major construction group in the region. Following this, he took on the role of Director at a reputed Chartered Accountants firm, where he has been instrumental in guiding businesses through complex financial landscapes.
