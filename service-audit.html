Professional Auditing Services

As Ministry of Economy (MOE) registered Auditors, we provide comprehensive auditing services for mainland companies and firms throughout the UAE, including Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ras Al Khaimah, as well as free zones such as Jebel Ali Free Zone (JAFZA), DMCC, IFZA, and Meydan Free Zone.

With over 10 years of experience as MOE-registered Auditors in the UAE, we have conducted audits across diverse sectors including Construction, Trading, Manufacturing, and Services. Frequently Asked Questions

How often should my company undergo an audit?

+

Most UAE businesses are required to undergo an annual statutory audit. However, the frequency may vary based on your company's structure, size, and regulatory requirements. We can advise on the specific requirements for your business.

What documents do I need to prepare for an audit?

+

Generally, you'll need to provide financial statements, ledgers, bank statements, invoices, contracts, and other supporting documentation. We'll provide a comprehensive list tailored to your business prior to beginning the audit process.

How long does the audit process take?

+

The duration varies depending on your company's size, complexity, and the type of audit being conducted. A typical audit can take anywhere from 2-4 weeks, but we'll provide a more accurate timeline after our initial assessment.

What makes your audit services different?

+

Our audits go beyond compliance to provide valuable business insights. With over 10 years of experience as MOE-registered auditors and extensive industry knowledge, we deliver audit services that help improve your business operations, not just fulfill regulatory requirements.
