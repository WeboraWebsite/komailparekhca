Frequently Asked Questions

How does the UAE corporate tax regime affect my business?

+

The impact varies depending on your business structure, industry, and operations. The UAE corporate tax applies to businesses with profits above certain thresholds, with different considerations for mainland, free zone, and small businesses. Our team can provide a tailored assessment of how the tax regime specifically affects your organization and what steps you should take to ensure compliance while optimizing your tax position.

Are there any exemptions or special rates available for my business?

+

Yes, the UAE corporate tax regime includes various exemptions, reliefs, and special rates for certain business types and activities. These include small business relief, free zone incentives, and certain industry-specific provisions. We can help identify which exemptions your business may qualify for and assist you in structuring your operations to benefit from available tax incentives within the legal framework.

How can I prepare my business for corporate tax compliance?

+

Preparation involves several key steps: assessing your current accounting systems, ensuring proper record-keeping practices, understanding applicable tax provisions, reviewing your business structure, and developing a compliance strategy. Our team can guide you through a comprehensive readiness assessment and help implement necessary changes to your financial and operational systems to ensure smooth compliance with UAE corporate tax requirements.

What are the deadlines and filing requirements for UAE corporate tax?

+

Filing requirements include registering for corporate tax, submitting periodic tax returns, and making tax payments by specified deadlines. The specific timelines depend on your financial year and when the tax became applicable to your business. We stay updated with all regulatory requirements and can ensure your business meets all filing deadlines and documentation requirements, helping you avoid penalties and maintain good standing with tax authorities.
